HOW TO PLAY

  1. Every shell is a function f(x). You see it from x = 0 to the TRIGGER at x = 4.
  2. Will f go below 0 between x = 4 and x = 8? Yes: call LIVE. No: call BLANK.
  3. LIVE: the riff falls under the axis into a distorted 808. BLANK: it stays up as glass bells.
  4. Solve it: find where f crosses 0 (roots, minimum, sin = −1…). The equation is under the graph.
  5. A right call pays 100 points.
  6. Right calls in a row multiply your payout: ×1 up to ×8. A miss resets it.
  7. Call right on a kick drum: ON BEAT, ×1.25.
  8. VS DEALER: LIVE shoots the Dealer, BLANK shoots yourself. A blank on yourself = shoot again.
