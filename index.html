HOW TO PLAY

  1. Every shell plays a riff. Call LIVE or BLANK before the TRIGGER.
  2. LIVE: the riff drops 2 octaves into a distorted 808. BLANK: it turns into glass bells.
  3. Count what's left. The math under the graph is your HUD: P(LIVE), your score Σ, your hearts ♥.
  4. A right call pays 100·ω, with ω = 1 / P(your call). Long shots pay more.
  5. Right calls in a row multiply your payout: ×1 up to ×8. A miss resets it.
  6. Call right on a kick drum: ON BEAT, ×1.25.
  7. VS DEALER: LIVE shoots the Dealer, BLANK shoots yourself. A blank on yourself = shoot again.
